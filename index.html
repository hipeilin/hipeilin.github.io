Publication

My PhD research designs human-in-the-loop visual analytics approaches for complex event sequence data, coupling algorithmic pattern extraction with interactive visualization so that analysts can uncover interaction patterns and relate them to higher-level constructs such as exploration strategies, learning processes, and reasoning. This work has been published at leading visualization venues, including IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics (IEEE VIS) and IEEE PacificVis.
